## 1. Conflicts Between Your Documents — Must Decide Before/While Verifying

Your 3 documents disagree on a few load-bearing points. Resolve these first — they change what "correct" looks like when you audit the build.

### 1.1 Android: Play Store or direct APK? — **CONFLICT, resolve now**
- Master Context doc plans to submit to Play Store ($25 dev account, AdMob banners).
- Earlier plan (this chat, pre-rename) explicitly said skip Play Store, direct-APK-only.
- **Current reality (verified):** Google Play's copyright/IP policy prohibits apps that facilitate downloading copyrighted content without rights — video downloaders in this category are routinely rejected/removed, this hasn't changed in 2026.
- **New wrinkle as of 2026:** Google is rolling out a developer-verification mandate — starting enforcement in select countries from September 2026, APKs distributed *outside* Play Store also need to come from a Google-registered/verified developer, or they get blocked on certified Android devices. Sideloading itself isn't going away, but anonymous/unverified APKs are getting harder to install.
- **Recommendation:** Don't submit to Play Store (rejection risk is real and well-documented). Do register as a verified Android developer with Google even though you're distributing outside Play Store — otherwise your APK may get blocked on newer Android builds from September 2026 onward regardless of your distribution channel.
- **Action:** Update Master Context doc's launch checklist — remove "Play Store developer account ($25)" as a store-submission line, keep it (or a Google developer-verification registration) as an identity-verification requirement instead.

### 1.2 macOS — dropped or still in scope?
- Original PRD (this chat) had Windows → macOS → Android as the order.
- Master Context doc's "3 Products" table has only Windows, Android, Website — no macOS.
- **Action:** Confirm explicitly: is macOS in v1, v1.1, or cut entirely? Nothing else in your docs (design system, file structure) mentions a macOS build, so treat it as **out of v1 scope** unless you say otherwise.

### 1.3 Windows backend: full Python or yt-dlp standalone binary? — **technical correction**
- Gemini's plan bundles a full embedded Python 3.11 runtime + a custom `downloader.py` script that shells out to `yt-dlp.exe`.
- **Verified current fact:** yt-dlp officially publishes a **pre-compiled standalone `yt-dlp.exe`** on its GitHub releases that needs no separate Python install at all — it's a self-contained binary.
- **Why this matters:** bundling an entire Python interpreter just to run subprocess calls to `yt-dlp.exe` is unnecessary weight (bigger installer, slower first-run, more surface area to break). You can call `yt-dlp.exe` directly from your Electron main process via `child_process.spawn`, parse the same stdout progress lines Gemini's plan describes, and drop `downloader.py` and the embedded Python environment entirely.
- **One real dependency you're missing:** current yt-dlp needs a **JavaScript runtime** (Deno recommended, or Node/Bun/QuickJS) bundled alongside it for full YouTube support — it's used to solve YouTube's signature/n-parameter challenge. Without it, YouTube downloads specifically can silently degrade or fail. Since your Electron app already ships Node.js, you're likely covered for this — but confirm yt-dlp is configured to use your bundled Node rather than expecting a system-wide install.
- **Action:** Verify your actual build — if it's shipping a full Python interpreter, consider ripping it out in favor of the plain standalone `yt-dlp.exe` + `ffmpeg.exe` + confirmed JS runtime access. If you've already built and tested the Python version and it works, this isn't urgent, just know it's carrying unnecessary weight.

### 1.4 Ads vs. Play Store policy risk
- AdMob rewarded ads to unlock playlist/subtitle/batch download is in the Master Context plan.
- Since you're not submitting to Play Store (per 1.1), AdMob account approval isn't blocked by the Play Store copyright policy — AdMob can still serve ads inside a sideloaded APK. Just don't conflate "AdMob approved" with "Play Store approved" — they're separate approval processes and you only need the former.
